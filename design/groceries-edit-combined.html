Calories and macros
Two things to look at while you tap.

1. The macro pills are the smallest targets on the screen. They are 9 px type in a 15 px pill, which reads well but is near the floor for a thumb. If they feel fiddly, they can become a third row of the figures block instead.

2. Save is a sticky bar, so it sits at the bottom of the sheet the moment you change anything. The alternative is committing each value as you tap Done on the pad, no Save at all. That is fewer taps but has no undo.
