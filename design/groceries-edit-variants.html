Groceries edit revamp

Issue #29, three directions for what a row tap opens

The problem. Today a row tap drops all ten blocks at once: nutrition lookup, label photo, name, brand, store, category, link, product photo, serving, my serving, four macro boxes, package weight, price. About eighteen controls.

The observation each direction reacts to. Lookup and label photo are creation tools. On an item that already exists you almost never touch them, yet they sit at the very top.

These are tappable. Open the bands, tap the values, hit Edit.
VARIANT A, LABEL
A tap opens a read only label, big photo, the two numbers that matter, macros. Edit flips the same card into fields. Lookup and label photo live only in the add flow. Costs one extra tap when you did want to edit.

VARIANT C, TAP THE VALUE
No form at all. The card is the editor: values are dashed, tap one and a small pad slides up with that single number, big, and nothing else. Save only appears once something changed. This is the one past your stated taste, it trades the familiar form for one thing at a time.

What is the same in all three. Nothing becomes uneditable, every field still has a home. The add flow (the + button) keeps its lookup and label photo up top, because when the item does not exist yet those really are the first thing you want.

Tell me which one, and what to steal from the others. Combining is cheap at this stage, for example B's bands with A's read only first screen.
